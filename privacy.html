Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Overview

Convergence Themed is a single-player real-time strategy game for Android. You play against computer opponents on your own device. This policy explains what data the game handles, where it is kept, and what your rights are.

Short version: The developer of Convergence Themed does not collect, receive, or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics, or multiplayer. Your settings stay on your device. If you buy the optional "Full Game" unlock, Google Play handles the purchase (see In-App Purchase below).

Data We Collect

The developer collects no data.

Convergence Themed has no servers of its own and contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking code. The developer cannot see how you play, what device you use, or who you are.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Game settings — your last match setup (factions, colours, number of opponents, difficulty, map size and similar options), display options, the selected theme, and which tips you have hidden.
  • Imported themes — theme files (JSON) that you choose to import with Android's file picker. The game reads only the file you pick and stores a copy in its private storage. It does not browse your other files.
  • Purchase status — a flag that records whether you own the Full Game unlock, so the game can start unlocked when you are offline.

This data contains no personal information. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Android backup: if you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include the game's settings in your Google account backup and restore them on a new device. Google runs this backup, not the developer, and you control it in your device's settings.

In-App Purchase

The game is free to play. An optional one-time purchase ("Full Game") unlocks larger maps, more computer opponents, and custom themes. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing:

  • Your payment details go only to Google. The developer never receives your card number, billing address, or other payment information.
  • When the game starts or resumes, it asks Google Play whether you own the Full Game, so that refunds and pending payments are reflected. For this, the game connects to Google Play services on your device, which talk to Google's servers.
  • Google shares order records (such as an order number, date, and price) with the developer through the Google Play Console, as it does for every Play purchase. They are used only for accounting, refunds, and support.

Google's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Permissions

Permission Purpose
com.android.vending.BILLING Lets the game offer the optional Full Game purchase through Google Play
INTERNET Added by the Google Play Billing library, so it can reach Google Play to check and complete purchases. The game itself does not connect to any other server
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Added by the Google Play Billing library, so it can tell whether the device is online

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files. Importing a theme uses Android's file picker, which gives the game access only to the single file you select.

Third-Party SDKs

  • libGDX — the open-source game engine the game is built on. It runs entirely on your device and sends no data anywhere.
  • Google Play Billing Library — handles the optional purchase, as described above. It does not include advertising or tracking.

The game includes no other third-party SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Convergence Themed does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no user-generated content shared with other players, and no ads. Purchases go through Google Play, which applies your Google account's parental controls and purchase approvals.

Your Choices

  • You can delete all game data at any time by uninstalling the game, or with Settings → Apps → Convergence Themed → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Imported themes can be removed in the game's theme menu, or by clearing storage.
  • To ask about a purchase or a refund, use Google Play's order history or contact the developer at the address below.

Changes to This Policy
