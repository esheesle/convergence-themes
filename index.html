The game

Real-time strategy, built for touch

Harvest, build and command whole fleets with taps, drags and pinches. Control groups, formations and a hunt mode for search-and-destroy.

17 factions, 3 regions

Every faction has its own scout, constructor, freighter, 8 warships, 8 stations and special abilities, from cloaking raiders to living, regenerating hulls.

Two resources and crew

Freighters mine crystal moons, mining stations work asteroid fields, and every ship needs crew that your base trains over time. Board an enemy ship to capture it.

Skirmish your way

Up to 3 computer opponents or allies with their own difficulty and teams. Five map sizes, adjustable resources, crew, fleet limits and game speed.

Make it your own universe

Themes rename and re-skin everything: factions, ships, weapons, resources, colours and art. Write your own, or let an AI write one for you.

No ads, no tracking

Plays fully offline. No accounts, no ads and no analytics. One optional purchase unlocks the full game.

How a match plays

1. Harvest

Freighters mine the crystal moons automatically. A mining station built beside an asteroid field extracts the second resource. Both run dry, so expand.

2. Build up

Shipyards build tier 1-2 warships, research unlocks tiers 3-4, and the advanced shipyard builds tiers 5-8, including one unique flagship.

3. Fight smart

Nebulae hide ships but drain their shields. Bases are fortresses with heavy armour and guns. Bring a real fleet, and use each faction's abilities.

4. Win

Destroy every enemy station and construction ship.

The factions

The built-in setting. Every faction plays differently, and every one is playable. A theme can rename all of them and redraw their ships.

Meridian Sector 5 factions

Concord of Worlds

Balanced fleets with strong shields, lance arrays and seeker torpedoes. Excellent repair support.

The Accretion

A machine collective that grows by absorbing what it defeats. Slow, massive and self-repairing, with cutter beams, grapple fields and the power to absorb crippled ships.

Shroud Dominion

Nearly every ship can veil itself. Plasma torpedoes, sensor-rich science ships and a flagship whose wither pulse kills whole crews.

Vardek Clans

Aggressive, tough and cheap. Veiled raiders, heavy ion cannons and boarding parties.

Saurak Throne

Massively armoured, slow brawlers with plasma weapons and vicious boarding parties.

Cinder Reach 5 factions

Helios Compact

Railguns, missiles and Sparrow fighter wings, upgraded with salvaged shields, beams and blast warheads. Hyperdrive jumps.

Luminar Wardens

Expensive but superb: zero-point shields and swarms of homing seekers. A floating citadel as a flagship.

Gloam Brood

Organic, unshielded and regenerating. Mite swarms, reaping beams that steal crews, and colossal brood ships.

Auric Satrapy

Cheap fighter wings, strong shields, lance cannons and elite boarding troops. Galleons launch fighters.

The Myriad

Cheap, fast and self-repairing. Ships spawn more Myriad swarms and infest enemy vessels.

Halcyon Rim 7 factions

Seren Covenant

Elegant crystalline warships with stealth fields that hide them from targeting, and devastating prism lances.

Frontier Coalition

Rugged, cheap warships with flak screens, pulse cannons, missiles and Lynx fighter wings. Big ships open jump gates.

Starwatch Accord

Fast Starwatch fleets built from elder technology: adaptive armour and their own jump gates.

Lumen Sovereignty

The oldest and most powerful: living ships with arc lances, regeneration and the world-ender. Very expensive.

The Thornkin

No shields, but enormous living hulls that regenerate. Rending beams, phasing and jump gates.

Corvane Republic

Ornate emerald-and-silver warships, Kestrel fighter swarms, ion cannons and mass-driver bombardment.

Kharast Dominion

Tough, cheap and brutal: heavy laser cannons, energy mines, Brute fighters and assault troops.
